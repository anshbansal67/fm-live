The IPL 2027 auction longlist has 1,220 players, and 30 of them have asked for the top base price of ₹2 crore. Cricinfo named 29 of the 30. We looked them up on the Fine Margins T20 ratings.

Three are in the top 25: Wanindu Hasaranga (20th of 1,133 rated bowlers), Matheesha Pathirana (23rd) and Shimron Hetmyer (24th of 1,074 rated batters). The rating is the points of win chance a player adds against an average IPL player, per match for a bowler and per innings for a batter, on one scale across every league he has played in.

Kolkata Knight Riders paid ₹18 crore for Pathirana at last year's auction. He played one match for them and was released this month. In IPL numbers (the same player run through today's IPL against average opposition) he goes at 8.86 an over, where the IPL average is 9.3.

Among the batters, only Hetmyer and Rahmanullah Gurbaz are in the top 100. Each rank comes with a range (Gurbaz could be anywhere from 13th to 209th), so players close together in a list could swap places. Mahli Beardman hasn't played enough T20 cricket to be rated yet.

Franchises send in their shortlists by 1 December. The auction is in Goa on 16 December.
