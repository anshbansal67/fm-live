England lead the T20I series 2-1 and can win it at the SCG on Sunday night.

Australia are playing the series without their three best-rated T20 bowlers on Fine Margins: Nathan Ellis (6th of 1,133 rated bowlers), Josh Hazlewood (7th) and Pat Cummins (14th). Cummins and Hazlewood were rested ahead of the Tests against New Zealand, and Ellis has a side strain. England rested their best-rated bowler as well: Jofra Archer (22nd), ahead of their Test tour of South Africa.

That leaves Adam Zampa (29th) as the best-rated bowler in either squad, with Adil Rashid (62nd) next. Jamie Overton was 113th before the Gabba, where he took 5-12 and Australia were bowled out for 108, their lowest T20I total at home. In IPL numbers (the same player run through today's IPL against average opposition) he takes a wicket every 18.4 balls, where the IPL average is 19.6.

With the bat, England have four of the six best-rated players in the two squads: Harry Brook (10th of 1,074), Aneurin Donald (23rd), Will Jacks (39th) and Phil Salt (43rd). Australia's best are Tim David (14th) and Mitchell Owen (25th).

The rating is the points of win chance a player adds against an average IPL player, per match for a bowler and per innings for a batter. Each rank comes with a range (Zampa could be anywhere from 17th to 121st), so players close together in a list could swap places.

The fourth T20I starts at 7:15pm in Sydney on 29 November.
