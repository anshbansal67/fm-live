The IPL retention lists are in. From the players each team kept, this is the best XI it can field, on one scale.

Mumbai Indians lead on +2.21 wins next season, and Sunrisers Hyderabad, Rajasthan Royals and Royal Challengers Bengaluru are less than a tenth of a win behind them. Delhi Capitals are 10th on +1.07, with ₹40.2 crore left and nine places to fill. Gujarat Titans are 5th with ₹3.75 crore left.

Best XI is the strongest side a squad can field under IPL rules: a keeper, seven who bat, five who bowl, no more than four overseas. Wins next season is the player's Fine Margins next-season forecast, batting plus bowling for anyone who bowls two overs a match or more, over a 14-match season against an average IPL player.

Purse left is the purse less the salaries reported for the players kept. The 2027 purse hasn't been announced, so last year's ₹125 crore is used until it is.
