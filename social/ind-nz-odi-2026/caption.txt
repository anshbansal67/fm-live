India won the ODI series in New Zealand 3-1. New Zealand scored 285 more runs.

India's first two wins were rain-shortened chases settled by DLS, and the Hamilton game was washed out without a ball bowled. Only the two games at Bay Oval went the distance. New Zealand won the first by 244 runs, and India won the last by 22 after being bowled out for 209.

Since 2002, two ODI series winners have been outscored by more: England against India in 2011 (by 335 runs) and South Africa in England in 2025 (by 331).
