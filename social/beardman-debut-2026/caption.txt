Mahli Beardman, 21, took 6 for 24 on his ODI debut in Perth as England were bowled out for 131.

Five of the six came in a 6.2-over burst that took England from 42/2 to 60/7. Only three other bowlers have taken six on ODI debut since 2006, and no Australian has bettered his figures in an ODI since the 2003 World Cup.
