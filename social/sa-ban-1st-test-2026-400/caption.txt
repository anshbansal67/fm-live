Bangladesh lost three wickets for 22 at the Wanderers on Sunday, all to Kagiso Rabada. By the 35th over they were 121 for 5, after Rabada bowled Tanzid Hasan and had Mushfiqur Rahim out first ball. On Monday they were bowled out for 400.

That is Bangladesh's highest Test total against South Africa, home or away. Their best in South Africa had been 320, at Potchefstroom in 2017.

Towhid Hridoy (137) and Litton Das (120) put on 206 for the sixth wicket, the highest stand by a Bangladesh pair against South Africa for any wicket. Hridoy is playing his fourth Test and has made 242*, 82 and 137 in his last three innings.

Rabada finished with 5/70 and Dane Paterson with 4/92.
