For 11 years the highest IPL total was 263. Since 2024, teams have passed it eight times.

Most people put that down to the Impact Player rule, so we compared the IPL with leagues that don't have it.

In 2022, IPL top orders scored about as fast as the top orders in the Blast, PSL, CPL and T20 internationals. In 2026 they scored 167 runs per 100 balls. Those four: 147.

Teams still use about eight batters an innings, only a fraction more than before, and batters 8 to 11 account for about a seventh of the extra runs. Our read: the extra batting mostly sits unused, but knowing it's there, everyone swings harder, the top three most of all.

Numbers from Cricsheet ball-by-ball data, men's T20, 2019 to 2026. Top order means batters 1 to 3; the T20 internationals are games between full members.
